About Anantham

The Mathematics Club of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore is known as Anantham, which means “infinity” in Sanskrit. The name was chosen because Mathematics gives space for everyone to understand concepts in numerous (infinite) ways, depending on how one perceives it and the background one looks at it from. Embracing this, the math-loving student community established Anantham as a platform for collaborative learning, where they can exchange experiences, engage in thought-provoking discussions, and nurture their passion for Math.

All students of Integrated MSc, MSc Mathematics, Integrated MSc Data Science, and MSc Applied Statistics and Data Analytics are members of Anantham by default. Students from undergraduate Engineering programs who wish to listen to or share observations on Mathematics and its applications are always welcome to join. We believe that interactions among disciplines foster a healthy academic culture, and help anyone who wishes to overcome mathophobia.

Concept demonstrations with models, problem-solving with mathematical software, discussions, lectures, mathematical games, and quizzes are common at Anantham events. The informal environment provides an avenue beyond the classroom and helps students imbibe qualities like cooperation, tolerance, and open-mindedness as they work in groups. Experts in various fields of Mathematics are invited to deliver lectures, and the History of Mathematics is celebrated through events on occasions connected to mathematicians.

This forum helps students appreciate the beauty and subtlety of mathematics, learn about the origin and development of its various fields, and keep up with recent developments that broaden and deepen their knowledge.

Dr. K. Gayathri, Assistant Professor (SG), and the Class Advisors of all programmes of the Department of Mathematics are the Faculty Coordinators of Anantham.
