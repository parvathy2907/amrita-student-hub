About Nature Club

The Nature Club of Amrita works with a mission to instill among the youth a love for plants and animals, developing respect for the oneness of life in all elements of nature and preserving the pristine beauty and purity of nature. The club involves students and staff in preserving the campus ecosphere and promoting an eco-friendly way of life through awareness camps, seminars and talks on environmental awareness.

Moments worth remembering

18 April 2023

Animal Rescue Warrior Award

EIACP, Ministry of Environment and Forest RP at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham (AVV) and the Nature Club of AVV organized an award function to honor women who have been at the forefront of animal rescue. Nine awardees from different walks of life — from the corporate sector, NGOs, retired professors and homemakers to housekeeping staff and domestic helpers, many working for stray animals with their own savings — were honored.

As part of Azadi ka Amrit Mahotsav and Mission LiFE, the event also included a cleanliness drive and a screening of the Oscar-winning film “The Elephant Whisperers”. Student ambassadors from the Amrita Animal Welfare Club and the Nature Club were recognised for their work in anti-rabies vaccination drives, feeding drives and caring for animals on campus.

Prof. C. Parameswaran, Principal Director of Corporate Industrial Relations, was the chief guest and emphasized the need for a society where animals can safely coexist with people. The program was coordinated by Dr. Maya Mahajan, coordinator of the EIACP Centre, and graced by Dr. Sankaran, Registrar. More than 300 students attended.

When we see Mother Nature as the embodiment of God, we will automatically serve and protect her.
18–20 September 2023

Eco-Ganesha and Kozhukattai (Modak) Making Contest

Vinayaka Chaturthi was celebrated at the Coimbatore campus with an eco-friendly Ganesha idol making contest and a Kozhukattai (Modak) making contest, giving students a chance to showcase their artistic skills while promoting sustainability. The idols were made from paper and waste potato powder, and campus decorations used paper flowers and leaves.

Winners

Eco-Ganesha: Ms. Dhanya V G and Ms. Sruthika A (First), Mr. Aayush M R (Second). Kozhukattai / Modak: Mrs. Radhika S and Ms. Sruthika A (First).
