About Srishti

The literary club provides a platform to showcase the literary aspirations and talents of students. It is an excellent forum for self-expression and the cultural aspirations of students. Poetry-reading sessions, debates, extempore speeches, quizzes, crossword games, book and film reviews, play enactments, and classic movie screenings are showcased at the forum. Besides English events, the club also organizes events in regional languages like Tamil, Malayalam, Telugu and Hindi.

The wings of Srishti include Peek-A-Book, Bhaava, Clarion, Dhwani, Debate, Kaleidoscope, Poetree, Quikreig and Udaan, as well as the Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam Literature wings.
