About Photography

The Photography Club tries to stimulate interest in creative photography. It organizes short-term courses on photography, conducts photo exhibitions and promotes amateur photography as a hobby among students.

Highlights

8 October 2022

Spot Photography: “Shapes and Shadows”

As part of the Gokulashtami celebrations, the Amrita Photography Club organized a spot photography event themed “Shapes and Shadows”, with 483 participants. Participants captured interesting shapes and shadows around campus and uploaded their photographs for judging.

The judges were impressed by the creativity and unique perspectives on display. First prize (Rs. 2500) went to Ms. Aparna Rajesh, second prize (Rs. 1500) to Mr. Rahul Menon, and third prize (Rs. 1000) to Ms. Neha Singh.
