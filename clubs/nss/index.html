About NSS

National Service Scheme (NSS) in India was started in 1969. This public service program is sponsored by the Government of India and is conducted by the Department of Youth Affairs and Sports. NSS is a voluntary program in which young people from colleges, universities, and also +2 level work towards creating a campus-community linkage.

NSS is beneficial to both students and society in many ways. It helps students grow individually and as a group. It makes students confident, develops leadership skills, and helps them learn about people from different walks of life. Students also learn other skills that help them lead a better life in various situations, and it teaches them to be disciplined and organized in working towards their goals.

The NSS symbol is based on the giant Rath Wheel of the world-famous Konark Sun Temple (the Black Pagoda) in Odisha, India. The wheel portrays the cycle of creation, preservation and release, and signifies movement in life across time and space. The eight bars in the wheel represent the 24 hours of a day. The red colour indicates that the volunteer is lively, active, energetic and full of high spirit. The navy blue colour indicates the cosmos, of which NSS is a tiny part, ready to contribute its share to the welfare of mankind. The symbol stands for continuity as well as change, and implies the continuous striving of NSS for social change.
