About Raagasudha

Raagasudha is a haven for music lovers at Amrita, where melodies rise beyond classrooms and rhythms echo through hearts. Founded to celebrate the power of sound and self-expression, the club welcomes students from all backgrounds — whether trained musicians or passionate beginners.

From soul-stirring ragas to upbeat fusion, Raagasudha encourages creativity, discipline, and cultural appreciation. Our events are not just showcases — they are experiences that connect performers and listeners alike. Every voice matters. Every note counts.

Join the Raagasudha music club and contribute to a vibrant community of artists, performers, and music lovers.
