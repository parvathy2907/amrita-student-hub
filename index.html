Life beyond the classroom

The best part of college often happens after the last lecture.

To further the overall development of students, Amrita encourages participation in a wide range of extra-curricular, social and community activities. Associations like Toastmasters, GDSC and Srishti, along with clubs like Elite, Anantham, Intel IoT and the Photography Club, bring out creative talent and build team spirit.

NSS and the Nature Club give students a platform for humanitarian and environmental work, on campus and beyond.

How to join

Three steps. Two minutes.

One form signs you up for up to three clubs. The coordinators take it from there.

Explore

Browse the clubs, look through their photos and find the ones that click.

Register

Fill in one short form with your details and pick up to three clubs.

Show up

Coordinators reach out about inductions. Turn up, meet people, make things.
