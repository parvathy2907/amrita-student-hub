Student clubs at Amrita

To further the overall development of students, participation in a wide range of extra-curricular, social and community development activities is encouraged. Associations like Toastmasters, GDSC and Srishti, along with clubs like Elite, Anantham, Intel IoT and the Photography Club, help bring out students' creative talents and promote team spirit.

Associations like NSS (National Service Scheme) and the Nature Club give students a platform to engage in humanitarian and environmental work.
